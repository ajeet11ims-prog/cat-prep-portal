Directions for questions 1 to 5

Refer to the data below and answer the questions that follow.

All the students of a class are standing in a queue facing North. Deepanjan, Indraneel, Pradeep, Pragyan, and Sumanta are the five tallest students in the class with distinct heights. All other students are shorter than them. The positions of these five students in the queue are such that they divide the whole queue into six groups, each having a distinct number of students. Among these six unique numbers, the smallest value is three.

Any one among the students can see up to the next taller student standing in front of him/her but not beyond that. For example, if Deepanjan is taller than Pradeep and Deepanjan is standing in front of Pradeep separated by 5 students standing between them (all shorter than Pradeep), then Pradeep will be able to see these 5 students as they are shorter than Pradeep and the person at farthest whom he will be able to see in front of him is Deepanjan. A student will be able to see everyone standing in front of him/her if all of them are shorter than him/her. None of these five students are standing at the terminal ends of the queue.

It is known that –
1. Deepanjan can see the front end of the queue and exactly eight students are standing in front of him.
2. The farthest person whom Sumanta can see is Pradeep. There are 15 students standing between them.
3. Pradeep can see that three students are standing in between Deepanjan and Indraneel. He can see a total of fourteen students standing in front of him.
4. The nine students who were standing at the rear end of the queue were standing in descending order of their heights from back to front and the farthest person each of them could see was Sumanta.
5. Indraneel can see that some students are standing in front of him and there is no one standing beyond them.
