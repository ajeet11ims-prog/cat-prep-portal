Directions for questions 1 to 5

Refer to the data below and answer the questions that follow.

Nine friends – Ajay, Biva, Chanchal, Deb, Elina, Falguni, Girish, Hetal, and Indra had spent a total sum of Rs. 2600/- to purchase movie tickets. The screening facility in the movie hall had a sitting capacity of 70 people. There were 10 seats located in each row. Exactly five different types of seats viz. 'Luxury' (@ Rs. 500/-), 'Prime' (@ Rs. 400/-), 'Premium' (@Rs. 300/-), 'Special' (@Rs. 200/-), and 'Ordinary' (@ Rs. 100/-) were available. Every seat number was a combination of row identifier and column number. Seats with column numbers 1 to 5 were located to the left of the aisle (the passage running from the front of the screen to the entrance at the back), and column numbers 6 to 10 were located to the right of the aisle. The rows were identified with the first seven letters of the English alphabet starting from back to front. The exact position of a seat was identified with the combination of the row letter, followed by the column number in that row; e.g., B-6 is the first seat located in the second row from back to the immediate right side of the aisle. Two seats in the same row situated on two different sides of the aisle should not be considered as adjacent seats. The layout of the sitting facility is given below through a diagram.


Further it is known that:

1. The nine friends bought tickets in four different rows. Exactly two of them were sitting alone in their respective rows.
2. Ajay, Hetal, Girish and Indra were sitting in the same row from left to right in that order. They were occupying three different types of seats.
3. Ajay and Elina were sitting in the same column in adjacent rows where Ajay was ahead of Elina.
4. The prices of the seats occupied by Hetal and Falguni were same.
5. Chanchal was sitting to the right of Falguni in the same row in which ‘Ordinary’ seats were available.
6. The prices of the seats occupied by Chanchal, Girish and Ajay were same.
7. Biva and Hetal were occupying seats of the same price and in the same column separated by one row in between them.
8. Hetal, Girish and Indra were the only group of friends who were sitting in three consecutive seats.
9. The seat located to the immediate left of Deb's seat was costlier than his seat.
10. The price of the seat just in front of Biva was different from her own seat.
All references to directions (e.g., left, right, front, back) have been made while facing the screen of the movie hall.
