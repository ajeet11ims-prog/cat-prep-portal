Refer to the data below and answer the questions that follow.

Kolkata International Film Festival of the current year has been scheduled by the organisers from 1st to 10th December. Among many venues selected for the festival, ‘Nandan Cinema’ has been selected for screening ten movies in ten days showing exactly one movie everyday. People can watch the same movie in one among the following three different time slots viz. matinee, evening, and night. The categories and subcategories of the ten selected movies have been shown below.



Ramesh, a prospective audience of the festival, asked his logician friend Mukesh about the schedule of the movies to be shown in Nandan Cinema. Mukesh, who already knew the complete schedule, replied in the following manner.
1. The languages of the movies scheduled to be shown on odd-numbered dates are either Hindi or English, only.
2. The languages of the movies scheduled to be shown on even-numbered dates are either English or regional languages only.
3. The languages of the movies scheduled to be shown on dates that are multiples of 3 are either Hindi or regional languages only.
4. Movies shown from 1st to 6th include all subcategories.
5. A movie of Non-Bengali subcategory has always been immediately preceded by a Hindi language movie in the festival schedule.
6. The movie scheduled to be shown on 6th was not a Bengali movie.
7. A Hindi Feature film, an English film of Indian sub-category, and a Hindi Documentary has been scheduled to be shown on three consecutive days in that order.
8. The movie scheduled on 4th is not an English movie of International sub-category.
