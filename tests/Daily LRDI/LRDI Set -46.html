Directions for questions 1 to 5

Refer to the data below and answer the questions that follow.

Five students named A, B, C, D, and E are in five different standards from Std. 1 to Std. 5, in no particular order. These students participated in an event conducted over 5 days. Everyday five different questions in Mathematics (one question each at the Level of Difficulty corresponding to the syllabus of Std. 1 to Std. 5) are kept on the table.

Students are sent to the table one by one and they pick a question out of the questions placed on the table subject to the following rules:
1. On day 1, day 3 and day 5, the students are allowed to search through the questions available on the table and they can pick any single question of their standard or below. However, if that question is not available because someone else has picked it and as a consequence, it has been removed from the table, they pick any random question to solve.
2. On day 2 and day 4, the students are not allowed to search through the questions available and they have to pick only one question randomly. Once a question is picked by a student, that question is removed from the table.

The students attempt to solve the question picked from the table. They can solve any question corresponding to the level of difficulty of their own standard or below i.e. the student in Std. 1 solves only the question for Std. 1. However, the student in Std. 2 solves the questions for Std. 1 as well as for Std. 2 and so on.

The following table shows the order in which the students were sent to the table on different days.
