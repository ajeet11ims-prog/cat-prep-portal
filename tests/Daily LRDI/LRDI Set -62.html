Refer to the data below and answer the questions that follow.


West Bengal Logistics (WBL) Pvt. Ltd. is an intra-state courier service provider which promises to deliver any consignment within the state in 24 hours. Geographically the state has two broad geographical divisions or ‘Zones’ – North Bengal and South Bengal. WBL has four hubs in North Bengal viz. Hilly, Dooars, Dinajpur, and Malda, and six hubs in South Bengal viz. Murshidabad, Nadia, Central Districts, West Border, Medinipur, and Kolkata (shown in the table). Each hub has 2 to 4 pick & drop points (PDP). The major cities in the respective hubs are considered as PDPs. For example, Kolkata hub has two PDPs – Kolkata and Howrah. A total of 27 such PDPs exist in the state.



As per the ‘Policy’ of the organization, every PDP will be served (i.e. a delivery person will come in a company owned vehicle to drop and pick consignments) at least twice a day – once in the morning and once in the evening, by the intra-hub vehicle of the respective hub, only.

WBL maintains a dedicated inter-hub vehicle for every pair of hubs and one dedicated intra-hub vehicle for each hub. Suppose, a consignment is to be sent from Kalimpong to Purulia. In that case, first it will go to the ‘Hilly’ hub using a dedicated intra-hub vehicle from ‘Kalimpong’ PDP. Then the inter-hub vehicle used for transportation only between ‘Hilly’ and ‘West Border’ will carry the consignment from Hilly to West Border hub. Then the intra-hub vehicle used for ‘West Border’ hub will pick it up and deliver it to ‘Purulia’ PDP.
