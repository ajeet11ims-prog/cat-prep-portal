Mrs. Noorani Kapdawala is the owner of a readymade garments store in Pune, India. Her store is known for good quality clothes – acquired from both domestic and imported sources. Clothes in the store can be classified into three types based on material used – cotton, synthetic and leather. The shop has two separate floors – the first floor is used exclusively for women’s clothing and the second floor is used only for men’s clothing. Mrs. Kapdawala is taking physical stock of the garments available in the shop on 31st March night to check whether the stock registers are properly maintained or not. Total stock of garments found in the shop was 5000 items. Here, each piece of garment has been referred to as an ‘item’.


Further, Mrs. Kapdawala got to know the following facts –
1.      44% of the total items were imported, while 33% of the total items were for men’s clothing.
2.      Non-leather items constitute 72% of the total items available and 5559% of them are cotton items.
3.      42.5% of the cotton items and 4678% synthetic items are imported.
4.      For women’s clothing, domestic leather items exceed the imported leather items by half the number of leather items for men’s clothing. Half of the leather items for men’s clothing are from domestic sources.
5.      The number of cotton items acquired from domestic sources for the women was 750. The number of Cotton items for men’s clothing were 100 less than that.
6.      Total domestic items found for women’s clothing were 1950.
